10. Job Application Tracker

PURPOSE
Help a job seeker organise opportunities and follow their application progress.

SCREEN
Status summary, opportunity cards and add/edit-note forms.

REQUIRED FEATURES
- List application cards; search company or role and filter status.
- Add a saved opportunity, delete it, and edit a short follow-up note.
- Update status to Saved, Applied, Interview or Closed using a select control.
- Show total, active (not Closed) and interview counts.

RULES
- Company and role: 2–80 characters; note: up to 160 characters.
- New entries start Saved; all listed status changes are allowed to correct mistakes.
- All employers are fictional; no real job listings or messages are sent.

STARTER DATA
Read data.json: it is an array of 5 objects. Field names: id,company,role,status,note. IDs identify source records. For new records, choose numeric IDs greater than the largest existing ID and increment them. New carts, plans and session registration lists start empty; the source catalogue remains separate.

ACCEPTANCE CHECKS (each starts from fresh seed data unless stated)
- Initial totals: 5 overall, 4 active, 1 interview.
- Change entry 2 to Interview: interview count 2.
- Delete entry 4: 4 overall and still 4 active; whitespace company is rejected.

OPTIONAL EXTENSION
Add an active-only filter.

Read START-HERE.txt and CSS-GUIDE.txt in the package root. Complete the required features before attempting the extension. All seed names, businesses, prices and records are fictional classroom examples.
